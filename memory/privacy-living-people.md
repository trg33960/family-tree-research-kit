The deployed site is public. For living people, include only what the user personally knows and the person is comfortable sharing publicly — never birthdates, addresses, or contact details. Mark living people `living:1` and keep their dossier minimal.

**Why:** publishing private details of living relatives is a real-world privacy harm and hard to undo.

**How to apply:** when in doubt, leave it off. Only use photos the user has the right to share. Treat everything on the page as public. See [[sourcing-standards]].
