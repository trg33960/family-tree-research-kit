Every name, date, and relationship placed on a card as fact must trace to a source recorded in that person's `src` array.

**Why:** a family tree is only as trustworthy as its evidence; unsourced guesses compound into a wrong tree.

**How to apply:** prefer primary records (certificates, census, parish registers, gravestones, passenger and military records). Treat a single hint as a lead, not proof — corroborate with a second independent record. Mark uncertain links clearly (e.g. a `PROBABLE` tag or explicit dossier wording); never silently upgrade a guess to a fact. Log which sources, logins, URL patterns, and record IDs worked in `research_notes.md`. See [[privacy-living-people]].
